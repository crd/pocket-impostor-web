Pocket Impostor

Support

One phone, three or more players, a few minutes a round. Everyone reads the same secret word — except one of you.

Something wrong, or an idea? Write to support@pocketimpostor.com. It reaches a person, and a real bug report gets a real answer.

If you are reporting a problem, the version number at the bottom of the title screen is the single most useful thing to include.

How a round works

  1. Enter everyone's names and pick a mode.
  2. The phone goes around the group. Each person holds the card down to read their word in private, then passes it on.
  3. Everyone gives one clue about their word out loud — enough to prove you know it, not enough to hand it over.
  4. Argue about it.
  5. Vote. If the group agrees, the accused is out and their role is revealed. A tie means a revote between whoever is level.

The three modes

Blind

Everyone gets the same word. The impostor gets no word at all and has to work out what everyone is talking about while pretending to already know.

Near word

The impostor gets a word close to the real one — Guitar against Violin. They can bluff convincingly, and often do not realise they are the impostor at first.

Decoy

Someone gets the near word but is not the impostor. They win by surviving the vote, which means the group can be right about who sounds odd and still be wrong about who to remove.

Common questions

Can I use my own words?

Yes. On the setup screen, choose to have someone pick the words — the way a person would write them on slips of paper. Hand the phone to whoever is sitting out, or enter them yourself before you start. You can play with only your own words, only the built-in ones, or both.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on the device. Aeroplanes and basements are fine.

How many people do I need?

Three at a minimum. It is better with four to eight — enough people that a wrong vote is possible.

Someone saw a card they should not have.

Start the round again. The app deliberately makes you hold a finger down to see a card, so an accidental glimpse is short, but there is no recovering a round where the word got out.

Is my data safe?

There are no accounts and nothing you type leaves your device. The full details are on the privacy page.

It crashed, or something looks wrong.

Please write to support@pocketimpostor.com with the version number from the title screen, your device, and what you were doing. Crash reports come through automatically, but they cannot tell us what you expected to happen.
